Your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Diamonds in the Desert is run by the CDO Autos program at Canyon del Oro High School and the CDO Auto Boosters, the booster club that receives all event funds. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you fill out our sponsor or vendor form, we collect your name, business name, phone, email, mailing address, website, business description, booth and payment preferences, and your typed signature. We also keep the emails you send us.
  • Payments. Payments are processed by Square. We never see or store your full card number. Square shares your name, email, phone, what you paid for, and any business name you enter at checkout with us.
  • Car registration. Vehicle registration is handled by CarShow Pro under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical information. This website doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own. Like any website, our web host (GitHub Pages) and our font provider (Google Fonts) receive basic technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, when a page loads.

How we use it

  • To confirm your sponsorship or booth and deliver what you signed up for, such as your logo on our website, signage, announcements, and booth placement.
  • To contact you about the show and your payment.
  • To keep the booster club's records, including receipts for tax-deductible contributions.
  • To thank sponsors publicly. Sponsor names, logos, and website links are published on this site and at the show by design.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that run the event:

  • Square to process payments.
  • Google, where form submissions are stored in a Google Sheet and Gmail account managed by the booster club.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to run the show and to keep the booster club's financial records, then delete it.

How we protect it

Access is limited to event organizers and booster club officers. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or online service is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we have about you, or to stop emailing you. Email diamondsinthedesertcarshow@gmail.com. We may need to keep some records for financial or legal reasons.

Children

Our forms are for adults and businesses. We don't knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Photos

Photos and video from the show may appear on this site and our social media. To have a photo removed, email us. See our Code of Conduct for details.

Other websites

This site links to other websites, including Square, CarShow Pro, the Town of Oro Valley, and our sponsors. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Email diamondsinthedesertcarshow@gmail.com, or write to CDO Auto Boosters, 25 W. Calle Concordia, Tucson, AZ 85704.
