Event rules

Code of Conduct

Last updated October 5, 2026

Diamonds in the Desert is a family-friendly, student-run car show hosted by the CDO Autos program at Canyon del Oro High School. Everyone who takes part (exhibitors, sponsors, vendors, volunteers, and visitors) agrees to follow this Code of Conduct.

Event organizers and student staff may ask anyone who doesn't follow it to leave. Fees are not refunded in that case.

1. Safety in the lot

  • Drive at walking speed (5 mph or less) when arriving, parking, and leaving.
  • No burnouts, revving contests, speeding, or exhibition driving, before, during, or after the show.
  • Follow directions from student staff and volunteers, and park only where you're directed.
  • Stay until the show ends at 2:00 PM unless you've arranged an early exit, then leave slowly and safely.

2. Respect people and vehicles

  • Treat students, volunteers, exhibitors, vendors, and visitors with courtesy.
  • Don't touch, sit in, or lean on a vehicle without the owner's permission.
  • Keep children close and supervised.
  • No harassment, threats, or abusive language.

3. A family-friendly school event

  • This is a school-sponsored event. No alcohol, cannabis, illegal drugs, tobacco, or vaping in the show area.
  • Keep displays, signs, and music family-appropriate. No vulgar or explicit material.
  • Keep music at a reasonable volume.

4. Exhibitors

  • You're responsible for your vehicle, its contents, and your display.
  • If your vehicle drips fluids, place a drip pan or mat under it.
  • Check in between 8:30 and 10:00 AM. Awards are presented at 1:30 PM, and judging decisions are final.

5. Vendors and sponsors

  • Check in at 9:00 AM and finish setting up by 10:00 AM. Don't set up or close early unless it's been arranged.
  • Sell only from your assigned space.
  • No fireworks, pornographic material, drug paraphernalia, or vulgar or explicit items or language.
  • You're responsible for your own booth setup, your sales tax (currently 8.7%), and an Oro Valley Public Market License.
  • Booth fees are non-refundable.

6. Weather

This is an outdoor event and happens rain or shine.

7. Photos and video

The show is photographed and filmed, including by drone, and pictures may appear on our website and social media. If you'd like a photo of you, your vehicle, or your child removed, email diamondsinthedesertcarshow@gmail.com and we'll take it down.

8. Taking part at your own risk

By taking part, you release Amphitheater Public Schools and CDO Auto Boosters from any and all known and unknown damages, injuries, judgments, and claims, as stated on our registration, sponsor, and vendor forms.

Questions about this Code of Conduct? Email diamondsinthedesertcarshow@gmail.com.
